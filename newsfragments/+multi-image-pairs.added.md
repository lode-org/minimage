`pairs_within_images`, `visit_pairs_within_images`, `cutoff_exceeds_half_width`, `image_extents`, and `ImagePair` enumerate every periodic image of a point set strictly inside a cutoff for small or skewed cells where `2 * cutoff` reaches or exceeds a periodic face altitude. The C and C++ ABIs expose `mi_image_pair`, `minimage_pairs_within_images`, and `minimage::pairs_within_images`.
